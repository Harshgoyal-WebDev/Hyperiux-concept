Hyperiux Website concept 01 · Scroll journey Sheet A-00 03.11.2026

Concept 01 · 9 chapters · 90 storyboard frames

Your brand is the cube. We build the world around it.

A scroll journey for hyperiux.com that explains how Hyperiux works the way an architect would. The visitor watches a site get surveyed, a plan get drawn, a structure go up, the client's identity become its facade, the systems get wired, and a finished district handed over. It is one continuous 3D world with no hard cuts, built around the Hyperiux logo.

A-01

The logo is already a building.

Two columns, a beam that climbs left to right, and a cube in the joint. Read as architecture, the mark gives us the whole story. Hyperiux is the structure, and the client's brand is the module it carries.

Module

The cube is the client's brand. It appears in every chapter: it floats on the empty site, unfolds into six brand facets, becomes the atrium in the plan, takes each client's colours, and finally rises in the central plaza.

Columns + beam

The H is Hyperiux. It is the frame that holds the brand up: strategy, design system and engineering. The cube has its own story, and the H is built around it.

12° rise

The beam climbs at about 12°, left to right. That is the scroll direction of the story, concept to conversion. The same diagonal becomes the user flow in the plan and the road through the project district.

One orange

#FF6A13 marks Hyperiux, and nothing else. It is used for the structure, markers, risers and highlights. Client colours only ever appear on the cube and the facade.

A-02

What we took from the five references.

Each reference gave one technique. Together they make a single world that moves from drawing to building to city, and from day to night and back to day.

A-03

Nine chapters, one camera, one day.

Light carries the pacing: drafting-paper daylight for thinking and planning, dusk for identity, night for technology and work, dawn for growth and handover. Every chapter ends on the frame the next one starts from.

A-10 → A-90

Storyboard: ten frames per chapter.

Each frame is a still from the same scroll timeline, sampled every ~11% through its chapter. Open any frame to scrub between them, or play the whole journey as a scroll prototype.

A-99

How it gets built.

The storyboard engine already works the way the site should: every chapter is a camera path plus scene state driven by one scroll value. Production swaps these SVG frames for a WebGL scene.

Stack

  • Next.js with React Three Fiber and drei for the single persistent 3D scene.
  • GSAP ScrollTrigger and Lenis for scroll, with one master timeline split into nine labelled chapters.
  • Logo geometry built from the real logo SVG paths with ExtrudeGeometry. The cube mesh never unmounts.
  • Headlines, cards and nav stay as DOM over the canvas, for SEO, accessibility and crisp type.

Look

  • Hidden-line drawing style: EdgesGeometry over polygon-offset fills (Heron, Enpower).
  • Night chapters switch to emissive edge materials on black (Zeggo) and instanced ribs with a physical material (Composites).
  • One post pass for paper grain, vignette and the day-to-night colour grade.
  • Instanced fins, buildings and particles keep draw calls low.
